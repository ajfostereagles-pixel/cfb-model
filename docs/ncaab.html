Tracked picks

Every pick is saved the first time the model makes it, with the line at that moment, and graded when the game finishes. Nothing is added after the fact.

Model accuracy

How well the model's own predictions did on past games, using only information available before each game. This is scores only, because past betting lines are not available for this sport.

Season by season

Picked the winner is how often the team the model favored won. Home team wins is the simple baseline of always picking the home team. Average miss is the typical error in the final margin.

Are the win chances honest?

Games grouped by the model's win chance for the home team. If the model is well calibrated, the actual home win rate matches the chance it gave.

What this does and does not show

Good at ranking teams. Picking winners better than the home-team baseline shows the ratings carry real information about team strength.

Not proof of an edge. Betting lines already include everything these ratings know and more. Beating a coin flip on winners is much easier than beating the line, and there is no history of past lines here to test against.

Tracked picks are the test. The Tracked picks page grades each pick against the real line, live. Wait for a few hundred before drawing conclusions.
