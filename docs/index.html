    The short answer

    How close is each model to the betting line?

    Error is the average miss on the final margin, in points. Lower is better, and the line's own error is the bar to beat. Gap is how far the model's number sits from the line. A correlation of 1.00 would mean the model simply is the line.

    Profit over time, all three models

    Each line follows one standard bet whenever the model and the line differ by 2 or more points. A line that sinks below zero is losing money.

    Does the model's lean mean anything?

    Games grouped by how far the model leans from the line. Left means it prefers the away team, right means the home team. Each bar is how often the home team covered, against a coin flip at 50%. A model with real skill climbs from left to right.

    The numbers under the bars are how many games fall in each group. Small groups bounce around, so do not read much into the tallest bars.

    Do bigger gaps win more often?

    Every game, grouped by how far the model sits from the line. If the model knows something, the bigger gaps should win more.

    Plain-English guide

    Betting line (point spread). Sportsbooks set a number of points to even out a game. If Indiana is favored by 7.5, Indiana must win by 8 or more for a bet on Indiana to win.

    Market-blind never sees the line. It rates teams from results, recent form and offense and defense ratings. Market-aware starts from the line and learns small corrections. Enhanced also knows how the market has priced each team lately and how its games run against posted totals.

    Pick. The side the model prefers when it differs from the line by 2 or more points. A bet pays about 91 cents per dollar when it wins and loses the dollar otherwise, so a bettor must win 52.4% of the time to break even.

    Profit and units. One unit is one standard bet. Profit is shown in units and in dollars if every bet were $100.

    Luck check. The chance that a model with no skill, winning exactly 52.4% on average, would do at least this well. Small numbers mean the result is unlikely to be luck. Checking many breakdowns makes some look good by chance, so trust patterns that repeat across seasons.

    Limits. One sportsbook line per game, which may not be the closing line. About 40% of games have a line. Real accounts also face betting limits and price moves before you can place a bet.
